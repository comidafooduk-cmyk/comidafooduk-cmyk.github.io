Delete your Comida account

You can delete your Comida account and its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Comida and go to Profile → Manage account.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, profile, saved and created recipes, recipe photos, plans, folders, shopping list, pantry and preferences straight away.

Can't access the app?

Email comidasupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we keep

Usage analytics are kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically. Any remaining data, such as recipe ratings, is deleted or anonymised within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
