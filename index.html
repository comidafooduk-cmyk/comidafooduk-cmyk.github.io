What Comida does

  • Build your week: pick lunches, dinners and breakfasts in about 5 minutes and see your plan's health score.
  • A shopping list that sorts itself: grouped by supermarket aisle and scaled to how many you cook for.
  • Cook from what you've got: add your pantry and find what you can cook right now.
  • Save recipes from TikTok and Instagram: share a video to Comida and it becomes a recipe card.
  • Over 120 recipes: student classics and recipes from creators, with nutrition, allergens and 5-a-day counts.
