Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

Comida ("we", "our", or "the app") is a meal planning and food tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you use Comida, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your email address and a unique user ID, used to create and manage your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • User-Generated Content: Meals you create, meal plans, shopping lists, saved recipes, and meal folders. This data is stored in Google Firestore and synced across your devices.
  • Photos: If you choose to add photos to your meals, images are stored securely. Photos are only accessed with your explicit permission.
  • Crash Data: Anonymous crash reports collected via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs and improve app stability. These are not linked to your identity.
  • Device Identifiers: Anonymous device identifiers used by Firebase for crash reporting. These are not used for tracking or advertising.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's core functionality (meal planning, shopping lists, recipes)
  • To sync your data across devices
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app performance
  • To display advertisements to non-premium users (via Google AdMob)

Advertising

Comida displays ads to non-premium users through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and general location data to serve relevant ads. You can upgrade to premium to remove all advertisements. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We also cache your data locally on your device using AsyncStorage for offline access.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Firebase/Google: For authentication, data storage, and crash reporting
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements to non-premium users
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data within the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of advertisements by upgrading to premium

Children's Privacy

Comida is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes through the app or by updating the date at the top of this page.
