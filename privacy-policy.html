Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Who We Are

Comida ("we", "us", or "our") is a recipe and meal planning mobile application designed for university students in the United Kingdom. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our app on iOS or Android.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account information: your name, email address and a user ID, created when you make an account
  • Your preferences: who you cook for, what you eat (for example vegetarian or vegan), foods you would rather avoid and your preferred supermarket, used to choose recipes that suit you
  • Content you create: recipes you save or write, plans, folders, your shopping list, your pantry, recipe ratings and the meals you log as cooked
  • Photos: pictures you choose to add to your own recipes, taken with your camera or picked from your photo library. We only access your camera or photos when you choose to add one
  • Usage analytics: how you use the app, such as screens visited, features used, searches made in the app and session length. This is linked to your user ID so we can understand how people use Comida over time
  • Crash and diagnostic data: crash reports, error logs, device model, operating system and app version
  • Device identifiers: an app instance ID used by our analytics, and your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), which our advertising partner uses to serve and measure ads
  • Approximate location: our advertising partner may estimate your general area (for example your town) from your IP address. We never collect your precise location

Links you share to Comida from apps like TikTok or Instagram are read on your phone to create a recipe. The link and video are not sent to our servers; only the recipe you choose to save is stored.

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as health information, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, or biometric data. Your diet choice (for example vegetarian) is used only to filter recipes.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save your recipes, plans, shopping list and pantry so they are available on any device you sign in to
  • To show you recipes that suit your preferences
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, using usage analytics
  • To find and fix crashes and errors
  • To serve advertisements, including personalised ads where you have given consent
  • To communicate with you about your account or respond to support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide you with the app and its features (e.g. account creation, saving recipes, your preferences and photos)
  • Consent: serving personalised ads using your advertising identifier, which you can grant or withdraw at any time
  • Legitimate interests: usage analytics and crash reporting to keep the app working and improve it, and showing non-personalised ads to keep the app free

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): to securely manage user accounts and sign-in. Data processed: email address, authentication tokens
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google LLC): to store your saved recipes, plans, shopping list, pantry and preferences. Data processed: account data, user-generated content
  • Firebase Storage (Google LLC): to store photos you add to your own recipes. Only you can access them
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): to display advertisements within the app. Data processed: device advertising identifier, approximate location (estimated from IP address), ad interaction data. Personalised ads are only served with your consent (see Section 6)
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Data processed: in-app events (such as viewing a recipe or adding a shopping list item), your user ID, an app instance identifier, device model, OS version and app version. It starts when you open the app and does not use your advertising identifier
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): to diagnose crashes and errors so we can fix them. Data processed: crash reports and stack traces, device model, OS version, app version
  • Expo (Expo Inc.): development and update infrastructure. Data processed: device type, OS version, app version for update delivery

These third parties process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them. Data may be transferred to servers located outside the UK, including the United States. Where this occurs, appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

6. Advertising and Device Identifiers

We use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Android's Advertising ID) and cookies or similar technologies to deliver ads and measure their effectiveness.

On iOS, we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before your advertising identifier is used for personalised ads. If you decline, only non-personalised, contextual ads are shown.

On Android, Comida shows non-personalised ads. AdMob may still use your Advertising ID to limit how often you see an ad and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the UK or EEA, Google may also show a consent message where you can choose how your data is used for ads.

Our analytics does not use your advertising identifier.

You can change your tracking preferences at any time:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads

7. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

We only share data with the third-party service providers listed in Section 5, solely for the purposes described in this policy. We do not share your personal data for independent marketing by third parties.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the app's services.

  • Account data (name, email): retained until you delete your account
  • Recipes, plans, photos, shopping list, pantry and preferences: retained until you delete them or your account
  • Usage analytics: retained for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically
  • Crash reports: retained for up to 90 days
  • Advertising data: managed by Google AdMob in accordance with their retention policies

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile > Manage account > Delete Account. This immediately deletes your account, profile, recipes, photos, plans, shopping list, pantry and preferences. Any remaining data, such as recipe ratings, is deleted or anonymised within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

If you cannot access the app, email comidasupport@gmail.com from the email address on your account and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

9. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: you can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: you can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure: you can request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing: you can request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability: you can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at comidasupport@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data protection rights have been violated.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure authentication via Firebase
  • Access controls limiting who can access user data
  • Regular review of security practices

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

Comida is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at the email address below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or the app's features.

When we make material changes, we will notify you by posting the updated policy within the app and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. We may also notify you via an in-app message for significant changes.

Your continued use of Comida after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
